MODULE 04 ✓ COMPLETE

Historical Delay Records & Punctuality

Historical-Delay-Records/

Mines multi-horizon historical run records (90-day & 1-year) to extract timetable recovery slack, bottleneck compounding, and Markovian delay transitions.

  • Sectional delay delta math: Δdelay = delay_arr(v) - delay_dep(u)
  • Identifies slack buffer sections vs saturated corridor bottlenecks
  • Markov delay state modeling (On-time, Minor, Moderate, Severe)
  • Autonomous overnight daemon supervisor with self-healing watchdog

Network-Anomalies/

Models stochastic disruptions that invalidate nominal timetable predictions, including signaling failures, emergency chain pulls, and outer signal hold-ups.

Indian Railways Reality

Domain Logic & Authority Systems

Real-world operational rules that dictate actual train movement across Indian Railways.

🛰️ CRIS & ISRO Systems

The system bridges operational telemetry feeds used across 68 railway divisions:

  • COA (Control Office Application): Section controller dispatching software plotting live train precedence.
  • RTIS (CRIS + ISRO): Locomotives carry GPS/GAGAN transponders transmitting real-time position every 30 seconds.
  • NTES: Public enquiry interface with 2–5 min latency.

🚦 Dispatching Precedence Hierarchy

When multiple trains compete for the same block section or platform, Section Controllers strictly prioritize:

  1. ARME / ART: Accident Relief & Medical Emergency
  2. Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, Tejas
  3. Premium Superfast & Duronto Express
  4. Regular Mail & Express Trains
  5. Passenger, MEMU, DEMU Locals
  6. Freight & Goods Trains (Container → Coal)

⚡ Braking & Environmental G&SR

Physics-based constraints governing acceleration and braking:

  • LHB vs ICF: LHB disc brakes stop in ~1,100m at 130 km/h; ICF shoe brakes require ~1,400m at 110 km/h.
  • FOG-PASS Rules: Visibility <600m forces speed reduction to 60 km/h (or 75 km/h with GPS device).
  • Timetable Slack: 5%–15% built-in recovery time before major junctions allows late trains to absorb delay.
