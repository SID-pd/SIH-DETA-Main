Layer 3: Dynamic Real-Time ML ETA Prediction Engine

Traditional railway inquiry apps take the departure delay at the previous station and project it forward linearly across the entire route. Layer 3 continuously forecasts station arrival times using machine learning, fusing real-time locomotive GPS pings, localized halt micro-weather, temporary speed restrictions, and Layer 1 behavioural weights into a probabilistic forecast.

Inference Engine

Multimodal Feature Fusion

Combines spatial track vectors, instantaneous train speed curves, localized fog density, and historical dwell DNA into a unified feature tensor.

Probabilistic Output

Confidence & Uncertainty Ranges

Rather than outputting a brittle single-minute guess, Layer 3 computes prediction variance ($P(\text{ETA})$) that automatically narrows as the train nears its destination.

Real-Time Refresh

Continuous Re-Inference

Recomputes arrivals every 30 seconds or upon significant telemetry events (abrupt deceleration, signal aspect drop, emergency brake application).

Static Extrapolation vs Dynamic Real-Time Adaptation

Static Timetable Extrapolation

Legacy Flaw
  • Train departs Kanpur Central 15 minutes late.
  • System blindly adds 15 minutes to all downstream arrival times all the way to Howrah (1,000 km away).
  • Blind to whether the train has a clear track ahead to make up time.
  • Blind to sudden river-basin fog slowing the train down to 30 km/h.
  • Passengers and platform staff receive inaccurate times, causing platform congestion.

RailPulse Layer 3 Dynamic Model

Modern Standard
  • Ingests real-time speed from GPS (42 km/h detected in fog valley).
  • Identifies exact length of fog restriction (KM 1020-1032).
  • Forecasts that delay will temporarily increase by 12 minutes, but Layer 1 buffer will recover 3 minutes before Prayagraj.
  • Updates Redis within 1.2 milliseconds for instant passenger visibility.
  • Station master at Prayagraj receives exact platform slot time, avoiding platform clashes.
