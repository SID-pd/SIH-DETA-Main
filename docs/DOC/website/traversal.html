Dual-Mode Railway Network Traversal Logic

Tracking over 13,000 daily trains across thousands of individual halt signals in microscopic detail at all times would overwhelm compute resources. RailPulse's core algorithmic innovation is Dual-Mode Graph Traversal: calculating aggregate section times during nominal flow, while automatically exploding into micro-halt inspection the instant delay variance or a localized anomaly is detected.

Mode 01

Normal Section Mode

Computes aggregate section-to-section transit times. Sub-millisecond compute cost, highly scalable nationwide across on-time trains.

The Trigger

Dynamic Anomaly Branching

Triggered when delay variance exceeds threshold (Δ > 3 min), or when hyper-local halt sensors report fog, heavy rain, or signal aspect drops.

Mode 02

Detailed Halt Traversal

Explodes the section into intermediate halts, signal posts, and sidings. Pinpoints the exact root-cause bottleneck and forecasts downstream recovery.

Operational Insight

Why Micro-Halt Isolation Prevents False Corridor Delays

1. Isolates Geography

River valleys (like the Ganga-Yamuna basin near Bindki) frequently experience dense fog while Kanpur and Prayagraj have clear skies. Mode 2 isolates the exact 12 km stretch rather than penalizing the whole 194 km.

2. Downstream Recovery Tracking

Because the system knows the fog clears after KM 1032, it calculates that the loco-pilot can accelerate back to 110 km/h between Fatehpur and Sirathu, recovering 3.2 minutes before Prayagraj.

3. Accurate Platform Slots

Station superintendents at Prayagraj receive an exact arrival window (+14 min instead of a speculative +45 min), preserving platform 1 for the Rajdhani without causing platform holding deadlocks.
