Real-World Operational Value

What Layer 1 Solves for Railway Dispatchers

1. Eliminates Unrealistic On-Time Timetables

Static timetables schedule a 2-minute halt for a 24-coach train at a junction with 4,000 boarding passengers. Layer 1 replaces this fantasy with statistical ground truth, preventing downstream signal pileups.

2. Predicts Recovery Ability Before Destination

Instead of assuming a 15-minute departure delay stays 15 minutes all the way to destination, Layer 1 calculates exactly how much buffer can be made up across each open track section.

3. Dynamic Seasonal Adaptation

Weights dynamically increase during Chhath, Diwali, and monsoon periods to reflect lower boarding speeds and damp track conditions without requiring manual timetable rewriting.
