Layer 2: Operational Constraint & Collision Detection Engine

Machine learning models are purely probabilistic—they can easily predict an arrival time that violates track separation rules or requires two trains to occupy the same section simultaneously. Layer 2 is the deterministic railway safety engine that enforces minimum headways, detects track occupancy conflicts, and generates precedence resolutions before any ETA is accepted.

Safety Rule 01

Headway Separation Enforcement

Enforces strict time and distance separation between consecutive rakes based on signalling block length, line speed, and emergency braking deceleration curves.

Safety Rule 02

Opposing & Interlocking Conflict

Detects convergent track conflicts at crossover junctions, calculating optimal holding patterns on loop lines to prevent gridlocks without causing domino delays.

Safety Rule 03

Platform & Turnaround Feasibility

Guarantees that predicted arrivals do not clash with occupied platform tracks, ensuring adequate time buffers for pit-line cleaning and rake maintenance crews.

Core Algorithms

The Three Pillars of Collision & Conflict Prevention

1. Headway Infringement

Rear-End Safe

Continuously calculates the instantaneous deceleration distance $D_{\text{stop}} = v^2 / (2 \mu g)$. If the distance between two trains falls below the emergency braking threshold, Layer 2 imposes a speed clamp on the trailing rake.

  • Considers wet rail track adhesion (drops from 0.38 to 0.22 during monsoons).
  • Enforces minimum 3.5-minute separation at all station entry home signals.

2. Platform Track Deadlock

Station Capacity

When two incoming trains are predicted to arrive at the same platform within 5 minutes of each other, Layer 2 checks alternative platform availability, line switches, and pit-line turnaround buffers.

  • Automatically diverts second rake to vacant loop platform (e.g. Platform 4).
  • Alerts station superintendent 30 minutes in advance for passenger guidance.

3. Converging Junction Lockout

Gridlock Safe

At high-density diamond crossings, two train paths intersecting at grade can cause complete gridlock. Layer 2 evaluates train priority rankings (Rajdhani > Superfast > Passenger > Freight).

  • Calculates lowest network-wide delay consequence before setting signals.
  • Prevents holding high-speed passenger rakes behind slow-accelerating goods trains.
Optimization Engine

How Layer 2 Solves Infeasible Predictions

When an operational conflict is detected, the engine evaluates candidate alternatives to select the mathematically optimal decision.

Candidate 1: Hold Trailing Train

Holds the trailing rake at the preceding station halt until the forward section clears. Evaluates total passenger-delay minutes incurred.

Candidate 2: Dynamic Rerouting

If the main corridor is blocked by unscheduled track maintenance, switches train to the loop line or parallel freight corridor track.

Candidate 3: Speed Regulation

Rather than stopping the train completely, instructs the loco-pilot via signalling to cruise at 50 km/h, timing arrival exactly as the platform clears.
